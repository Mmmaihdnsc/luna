TERRA-LUNA: Planetary Surface Analog Discovery & Characterization Engine

Challenge: Identify Earth Locations that Analog the Permanent Moon Base Locations and Mars.

Project Summary

Selecting permanent lunar base sites (e.g., Artemis South Pole Shackleton Rim, Marius Hills Lava Tubes) and Mars landing zones (Jezero Crater, Utopia Planitia) requires validating critical hardware and survival protocols before launch. TERRA-LUNA harnesses open Earth, Moon, and Mars orbital datasets from NASA (LRO LOLA, Diviner, MRO CRISM/HiRISE, Earthdata SRTM/MODIS/EMIT) to discover, cross-reference, and characterize terrestrial analog sites across volcanic lava tubes, hyper-arid salt flats, high-arctic impact craters, and polar deserts. Our platform computes a multi-parametric 5-axis Planetary Similarity Index (Topography, Mineralogy, Environmental Extremes, Regolith Mechanics, and Mission Utility) and delivers an interactive geospatial intelligence dashboard with side-by-side telemetry, topographic transect simulators, and exportable mission briefing dossiers.

Core Capabilities & Innovation

  • Quantitative 5-Axis Analog Index: Replaces subjective comparisons with mathematical scoring grounded in NASA LOLA, MOLA, and CRISM spectral data.
  • Interactive Geospatial Explorer: Dark-mode NASA telemetry map linking Earth analogs directly to Artemis base candidates and Mars exploration zones.
  • Topographic Transect Simulator: Real-time cross-section slope roughness and obstacle evaluation for rover traverse planning.
  • Dual-Body Telemetry Matrix: Direct head-to-head parameter comparison with live radar charts.
  • Mission Briefing Dossier Export: Instant PDF/printable briefing sheets for field mission teams and mission planners.
