The Problem • Mission Risk

Why Terrestrial Analogs are Crucial Yet Under-Characterized

Problem 01
High Mission Stakes
  • Artemis & Mars missions require touchdown on extreme terrain (polar craters, lava tubes, buried ice).
  • Hardware failure on the Moon or Mars is catastrophic and irreversible.
  • Rovers, seals, and drills must be validated in terrestrial analogs prior to flight.
Problem 02
Fragmented Data
  • Historical selection was qualitative: 'it visually looks like Mars'.
  • Terrestrial satellite archives (ASTER, Landsat) were disconnected from planetary data (LOLA, CRISM).
  • No standardized quantitative similarity metric existed across missions.
Problem 03
Operational Blind Spots
  • Subsurface permafrost stability and cryogenic volatile extraction remain under-tested.
  • Razor-sharp glassy tephra damages rover wheels and spacesuit seals.
  • Subterranean lava tube bases lack unified structural catalogs.

Scientific Methodology • Algorithmic Scoring

The 5-Axis Planetary Similarity Index

The Formula
Score = w₁·S_topo + w₂·S_min + w₃·S_env + w₄·S_reg + w₅·S_util

Calculates normalized 0–100% fidelity sub-indices derived directly from orbital telemetry and ground-truth physics.

Top Verified Matches:
• McMurdo vs Utopia Planitia: 96%
• Atacama vs Jezero Crater: 95%
• Askja vs Marius Hills Pit: 94%
• Kilbourne Hole vs Artemis Rim: 93%
Core Dimensions Evaluated
  • 1. Topography & Slope: Elevation variance, roughness RMS, and slope gradients (LOLA/MOLA vs ASTER GDEM).
  • 2. Mineralogical Fidelity: Absorption spectroscopy for basalt, clays, sulfates, and opal-A silica (CRISM vs Landsat/EMIT).
  • 3. Environmental Extremes: Thermal range swings (-246°C to +50°C), aridity index, and UV radiation (Diviner vs MODIS).
  • 4. Regolith Mechanics: Grain size distribution, cohesion, and wheel sinkage friction angles.
  • 5. Mission Subsystem Utility: Operational fit for EVA suits, rovers, ISRU drills, or cave shelters.

Open Science • Data Provenance

Powered by 100% Open Data from Space Agencies

NASA Lunar Science
LRO & GRAIL
  • LRO LOLA: Topographic DEMs & surface slope roughness models.
  • LRO Diviner: Lunar thermal radiometer scans of PSR cold traps.
  • LROC ShadowCam: Ultra-sensitive imaging of shadowed craters.
  • GRAIL: Subsurface density mapping for lava tube caverns.
NASA Mars Exploration
MRO, Curiosity, M2020
  • MRO CRISM: Hyperspectral visible/IR mineralogy cubes.
  • MRO HiRISE: Sub-meter imagery of active dunes and scarps.
  • MRO SHARAD: Shallow radar sounding of subsurface ice.
  • CheMin & SuperCam: Ground-truth mineralogical verification.
Earth Observation & Partners
Earthdata, ESA, JAXA
  • NASA Earthdata: MODIS LST, ASTER Global DEM, Landsat 8/9.
  • NASA EMIT: ISS 285-channel mineral dust spectrometer.
  • ESA Copernicus: Sentinel-2 10m multispectral imagery.
  • ESA & JAXA: PANGAEA astronaut cave training and Kaguya LRS.
